Data Masking Template
Prepare your numbers before the AI audit
Convert your real figures into anonymised amounts before sharing with any AI tool. Your bank details stay private. The AI gets everything it needs.
🔒
How it works: Write your real figure on the left. Round it and remove identifying details. Use the anonymised figure in AI prompts. The AI never needs the exact number. It needs the pattern.

$4,247.83/month from Deloitte → $4,200/month from my employer — rounded, employer removed

$18,432 HECS debt → $18,500 student debt — rounded, type genericised
⚠️
Never share with AI: account numbers, BSB/routing numbers, passwords, Medicare or Social Security numbers, passport details, or login credentials of any kind. The AI is the architect of your plan — not a platform with access to your accounts.
When complete: use your anonymised figures inside the Audit prompts in Chapter 3. You now have everything the AI needs to build an accurate picture of your financial reality.
